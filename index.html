Service Campaign 25T-A04: Rear Leaf Spring Inspection

Campaign ID: 25T-A04  |  Issued February 2026  |  Reference: NHTSA-25V-0142
Action recommended: Owners of select 2020–2023 Tacoma vehicles are advised to schedule an inspection at a participating Toyota dealer. There is no charge for this service.

Affected Vehicles

  • 2020–2023 Toyota Tacoma (SR, SR5, TRD Sport, TRD Off-Road, Limited)
  • Production date range: 03/2019 – 11/2022
  • Vehicles equipped with factory leaf-spring rear suspension

Concern

In a small number of vehicles within the affected production range, the rear leaf-spring shackle bracket may exhibit accelerated corrosion in environments with high road-salt exposure. In an extreme case, this could result in reduced rear suspension performance and unexpected vehicle handling characteristics.

What Toyota Will Do

Authorized Toyota dealers will perform a complimentary visual inspection of the rear leaf-spring assembly. If wear or corrosion is identified beyond service limits, the affected components will be replaced at no cost to the owner. The inspection is expected to take approximately 30 minutes.

What Owners Should Do

Owners may schedule an inspection through the Toyota Owner's portal or by contacting their preferred Toyota dealership. Please reference Campaign 25T-A04 when scheduling.
